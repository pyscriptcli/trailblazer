# Experience Cloud TrailMix

**Type:** Trailhead TrailMix study guide
**Checked:** 2026-09-28

## What this collection covers

This TrailMix moves from Experience Cloud fundamentals through site customization, customer and partner site setup, audience targeting, knowledge, and a Financial Services example. The current collection is approximately 10 hours 35 minutes across eight resources. Experience Cloud sites provide an external-facing experience connected to Salesforce; the right access model depends on audience, licenses, and org configuration.

## Practical implementation sequence

1. Define the audience and their tasks before selecting site features.
2. Confirm licenses, identity needs, and how external users will be provisioned.
3. Design object and record access before exposing data through pages or components. Review sharing, profiles/permission sets, and guest-user exposure carefully.
4. Configure and brand the experience, then add the relevant customer or partner capabilities.
5. Test as representative external users, including access to records, files, knowledge, and service features.
6. Use audience targeting only after establishing safe base access; personalization changes what people see but should not be treated as the security boundary.

## Review prompts

1. Who is the site audience, and which records should each person access?
2. What access is provided by the license and what is controlled through sharing and permissions?
3. How will you test a site's experience as an external user?
4. Which content is personalized, and what security controls protect it independently?
