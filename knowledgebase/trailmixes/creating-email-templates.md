# Creating Email Templates TrailMix

**Type:** Trailhead TrailMix study guide
**Checked:** 2026-09-28

## What this collection covers

This TrailMix is a curated set of Marketing Cloud Engagement learning resources about composing, sending, personalizing, and managing email content. It is distinct from Lightning email templates used in the Salesforce CRM interface; see [the CRM template guide](../articles/salesforceben-lightning-email-templates.md) for that separate feature.

The current collection lists six resources, totaling roughly six hours:

1. [Email Creation and Sending](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/email-creation-and-sending) (30 min): email creation and sending foundations.
2. [Email Marketing Strategies](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/mr-email-marketing-strategies) (1 hr 15 min): planning email programs and applying marketing practices.
3. [Marketing Content Creation](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/marketing-content-creation) (25 min): creating reusable campaign content.
4. [Quick Start: Create and Send an Email with Marketing Cloud Engagement](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/quick-start-create-and-send-an-email-with-marketing-cloud) (45 min): an introductory create-to-send workflow.
5. [Create Compelling Content with Content Builder](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/trails/create-compelling-content-with-content-builder) (2 hr 20 min): working with Content Builder and its content assets.
6. [Personalized Email Marketing](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/modules/personalized-email-marketing) (40 min): adapting messages for audience context.

TrailMix membership and estimates may change; consult the [original TrailMix](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/users/adesrosiers/trailmixes/creating-email-templates) for its current contents.

## Suggested study sequence

Start with the email creation and sending module. Learn the overall marketing strategy and content-building resources before producing reusable material. Complete the quick-start workflow to connect those concepts, then study personalization and consider how audience data, content choices, and sending practices work together.

## Practical checklist

- Identify the intended audience and purpose before composing.
- Keep the message and reusable assets consistent with the campaign goal.
- Review the selected audience and personalization inputs before sending.
- Preview and test the message in the available Marketing Cloud workflow.
- Check the current product setup and permissions in the target account; capabilities vary by edition and configuration.

## Review prompts
