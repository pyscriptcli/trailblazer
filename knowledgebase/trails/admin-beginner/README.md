# Admin Beginner — Study Roadmap

**Official trail:** [Admin Beginner](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/content/learn/trails/force_com_admin_beginner)  
**Trailhead estimate:** About 7 hours 20 minutes · 5 badges · Foundational Administrator

This roadmap summarizes the full trail as it appeared on 2026-09-28. The trail teaches an admin workflow: shape data, move data safely, tailor the interface, guide users, and turn records into useful insight. Follow Trailhead for its live challenges and org-specific setup requirements.

## A practical sequence

1. **Model the data:** decide what records you need and how they relate.
2. **Load and maintain it:** clean the source, map fields, and verify import/export results.
3. **Make work easy to do:** put the right objects, fields, and actions in the user's workflow.
4. **Teach in context:** provide concise guidance at the moment it helps.
5. **Measure outcomes:** use reports and dashboards that answer agreed business questions.

## Trail-wide review

- Can you explain object, field, record, and relationship in plain language?
- Which import/export tool fits the record volume and object/workflow requirements?
- Can you distinguish a Lightning record page, page layout, compact layout, and list view?
- What makes in-app guidance useful instead of disruptive?
- Can you turn a business question into a report type, filters, groups, and a visualization?
